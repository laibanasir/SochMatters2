About Soch Matters

Where Every Thought Matters

Soch Matters was created with a simple belief: people deserve to be heard.

Mental wellbeing is deeply personal, and no two people experience life in exactly the same way. Soch Matters aims to create a safe, accessible, and human-centred space where people can seek professional support, talk openly, and find guidance without fear of judgment.

We bring together counselling, therapy, and consultation services through an accessible online platform, making it easier for people in Pakistan and beyond to connect with the right support.

Our goal is not simply to provide a service. It is to build a culture where talking about what you are going through is accepted, seeking help is encouraged, and mental wellbeing is treated as an important part of life.

Meet Hiba Khan

The Brain & Heart Behind Soch Matters

Hiba Khan

CEO & Founder — Soch Matters

The brain behind the vision. The heart behind the mission.

Hiba Khan is the CEO and driving force behind Soch Matters.

She is the person who envisioned Soch Matters as more than just an online counselling platform. Her vision is to create a space where people feel seen, heard, respected, and understood.

As the brain behind Soch Matters, Hiba leads its vision, direction, and development — shaping how the platform serves people and how it can make professional support more accessible.

As its heart, she brings the empathy and human connection that sit at the centre of the Soch Matters philosophy.

Hiba believes that sometimes people don't need someone to immediately solve their problems. They first need someone willing to listen.

That belief is at the heart of Soch Matters.

Hiba's Vision

A safe space to talk

“Everyone deserves a safe space to talk, without judgment and without fear.”

Through Soch Matters, Hiba wants to help create a world where reaching out for support is not something people feel ashamed of, but something they feel empowered to do.

Her vision is to build Soch Matters into a trusted platform for mental wellbeing and professional consultation — connecting people with the support they need, when they need it.

Because sometimes, being heard is the first step toward feeling better.

For Anyone. Anytime. Anywhere. Professional support, guidance and consultation in a safe, confidential environment.
